# Evidence verification

Generated by `evidence_verification.py`. Every figure below is computed, none typed.

What is checked, mechanically, for every line of "Why this conclusion?" and every quotation: the review exists; the quotation is a verbatim slice of it at the stated position; the review ids are unique, valid and as many as the count; the count is reproduced by reading the reviews again; no review counts for both praise and complaint of one aspect; no two counted reviews have the same text; the aspect belongs to the product's profile; no reviewer field is in the payload.

**Not checked here**: whether the aspect is the right one for the sentence and whether the direction is right. That is judgement. `precision_check.md` and `aspect_validation.md` record the developer's informal judgements and `human_evaluation.md` asks for independent ones.
