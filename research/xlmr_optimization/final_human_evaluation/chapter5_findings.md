# Chapter 5: findings (restricted to measured results; reference standard is AI-assisted)

1. The shipped XLM-RoBERTa sentiment classifier reproduces its published FiReCS score (accuracy and macro F1 0.833, 3,147 reviews).
2. Evaluation used a frozen 400-review marketplace test whose reference is two AI-assisted annotation sets (ChatGPT, panel- and adviser-approved, policy v1). The two sets agree on 91.6% of three-class labels (kappa 0.84, descriptive only: they are not independent annotators), disagreeing mainly where a feature list or generic praise is positive or neutral (31 of 37 disagreements).
3. On the 351 reviews where both sets agree, the shipped model scores macro F1 0.581 and an optimised head on the same encoder 0.653; accuracy 0.741 and 0.826; neutral F1 0.139 and 0.194. The 0.80 target for macro F1 and for neutral F1 is not achieved.
4. The shortfall is concentrated in the neutral class: most neutral reviews are predicted positive, consistent with training labels that follow a different neutral definition (an older AI rubric with 2% neutral, FiReCS convention, marketplace team labels) from the final-test rubric.
5. The reference is AI-assisted and becomes verified ground truth only after human adjudication of the 37 disagreements and a documented human check of agreed rows. Remaining work: record the annotation process, label a development set under policy v1 with enough neutral examples, retrain with grouped cross-validation, then evaluate under a pre-approved protocol.
