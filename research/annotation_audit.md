## How to use this

1. Open the CSV, sort by `priority`, and adjudicate the rows that matter for the five flags.
2. Record the decision in a new column; keep the original label.
3. Where the decision depends on the listing (`needs_listing_context` = yes), check the listing before deciding.
4. Decisions that repeat across many rows belong in the policy (`research/annotation_policy_proposal.md`), not in row-by-row edits.
